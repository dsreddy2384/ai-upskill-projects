# Idea check: "Why does nobody use my app?" report (2026-11-27)

**Verdict: killed as a product (17/25), with one narrow angle worth a cheap test.** The pain is real and recurring, but the market is crowded at very low prices, and the best competitor is free: other people in the same forums.

## Evidence (last 30 days)

- **Shopify Community:** 7 "traffic but no sales" or "roast my product page" threads since 30 October, with 18 to 39 replies each. Examples: [75 add-to-carts but 0 sales](https://community.shopify.com/t/need-feedback-75-add-to-carts-but-0-sales-what-stops-first-time-visitors-from-buying/714048) (39 posts), [Australian stores getting traffic but not enough sales](https://community.shopify.com/t/australian-shopify-stores-getting-traffic-but-not-enough-sales-need-honest-expert-feedback/717576) (31 posts), [new UK jewellery brand, clicks but almost no sales](https://community.shopify.com/t/roast-my-product-page-new-uk-jewellery-brand-getting-clicks-but-almost-no-sales/709248) (34 posts).
- **App and SaaS founders:** thin in readable sources. Hacker News returned mostly unrelated uses of "no users"; Indie Hackers and Reddit are blocked from this environment. One HN post asked "how do I get out of the 0 users cage" ([dead post](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50309004)).

## What the free forum replies already do well

The replies diagnose causes a page scanner misses:
- The same product sold elsewhere for far less ("the hand warmer thing is 30 bucks on a site I just looked at").
- A copycat store with the same images and a lower price.
- Delivery of 9 to 12 business days hidden behind "Australian Owned & Operated".
- A store name that suggests a different product.
- Ads landing on the homepage instead of the product shown.
- Return policy on the product page contradicting the refund policy.

## Score

Frequency 4, intensity 3, willingness to pay 2, reachability 4, buildability 4 = **17/25**, below the bar.

## The one angle not covered

Page scanners judge the page. The forum replies that helped most looked outside it: what the same product costs elsewhere, copycats, delivery promises, ad-to-page mismatch, contradictions between pages. A "market reality check" that does this would be different, but its buyers are often dropshippers with thin budgets, and the honest answer is often "your product or price is the problem", which is hard to sell.

## Cheapest test, if pursued

Answer 5 of these threads in the Shopify Community using the outside-the-page checklist, free. Count how many owners reply asking for more, and whether any offers to pay. Under 2 of 5: drop it.
