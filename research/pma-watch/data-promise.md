# What happens to documents you share

Plain-language commitments for the free first audits. Send this before asking anyone for documents.

1. **One use only.** I use your documents to prepare your audit and nothing else. I don't sell, publish or share them.
2. **Deleted in 30 days,** or sooner if you ask. I'll confirm by message when they're gone.
3. **I never contact your manager.** You get the findings and a draft email; whether and when to send it is up to you.
4. **AI is involved.** The documents are read with Claude, an AI tool made by Anthropic, and I check its findings by hand. If you'd rather the documents never go into an AI tool, say so and I'll do it by hand.
5. **Never logins.** I will never ask for your owner-portal login, bank details or passwords.
6. **You control the sharing.** Share through a folder you can revoke, such as a Google Drive link, rather than by email.
7. **Blacked out is fine.** See the redaction guide; I don't need names or addresses.
8. **Anonymous write-up only with permission.** If I write up what I learn from the first audits, it will have no names, addresses, company names or amounts that could identify you, and I'll ask you first.
9. **Not legal advice.** The report lists differences to ask your manager about. If something looks serious, talk to a local attorney.
