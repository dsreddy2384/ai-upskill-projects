# SAMPLE PMA Watch audit report

> **Made-up example.** It checks `sample-statements.md` against `sample-agreement.md`, both fictional. It shows what an owner would receive. It is not legal advice. Every finding is a difference to ask the manager about, not a conclusion that the manager broke the agreement: there may be approvals, notices or reports the owner hasn't seen.

**Property:** 1418 Example Avenue, Units A and B
**Period checked:** August to October 2026 (3 statements)
**Agreement clauses checked:** fees (4), approvals (5), owner payments (6), inspections (7), renewals (8)

## Summary

| | |

## Findings, most important first

### 1. A water heater was repaired, then replaced five weeks later

- **Statements show:** $640 "routine" repair on 9 September, then a $1,380 "emergency" replacement on 14 October. Same unit (A), $2,020 in total plus fees.
- **Agreement says:** emergencies may go ahead without approval, but the owner must be notified within 24 hours (5.3).
- **Ask:** the 24-hour notice for the October replacement; both vendor invoices; the age of the old heater; and whether replacing it in September was considered.

### 2. A repair over the $400 limit with no approval on record

- **Statements show:** the $640 September repair is marked "routine", not emergency.
- **Agreement says:** repairs over $400 need written owner approval before work starts (5.2).
- **Ask:** a copy of the approval. If there wasn't one, how will approvals be handled from now on?

### 3. Inspection fees charged for tenant checklists — $150

- **Statements show:** "Semi-annual inspection, tenant-completed checklist", 2 × $75.
- **Agreement says:** the manager's own staff inspect each unit's interior, and a photo report reaches the owner within 7 days (7.1, 7.2).
- **Ask:** the photo reports. If staff did not inspect, ask for a refund of $150 and dates for proper inspections.

### 4. Management fee charged on rent that wasn't collected — $49.50

- **Statements show:** September fee $202.50, which is 9% of the full $2,250. Only $1,700 was collected.
- **Agreement says:** 9% of rent actually collected (4.1). That is $153.00.
- **Note:** October's fee correctly includes the $550 late balance, so in September the fee was charged early on money not yet received. Ask for $49.50 back.

### 5. Coordination fee at 15% instead of 10% — $32.00

- **Statements show:** $96.00 on a $640 invoice (15%).
- **Agreement says:** 10% of the vendor invoice (4.4), which is $64.00.
- **Ask:** for $32.00 back. The August and October fees were correct at 10%.

### 6. Renewal signed with no recommendation on record

- **Statements show:** Unit B renewed for 12 months at the same $1,100, with a $150 fee, on 20 October.
- **Agreement says:** a recommended rent with at least three comparable rents, sent 60 days before the lease ends, and owner approval before signing (8.1, 8.2).
- **Ask:** for the recommendation and the comparable rents that were sent. This report does not check market rent.

### 7. September payment 12 days late

- **Statements show:** paid to owner on 24 September.
- **Agreement says:** by the 12th (6.2).
- **Ask:** the reason, and that payments arrive by the 12th.

## What this audit could not check

- Approvals or notices given by phone or text.
- Whether inspections happened at all, beyond the statement wording.
- Whether $1,100 was the right renewal rent for the area.
- The security deposit and the $500 reserve, which the statements don't show.
