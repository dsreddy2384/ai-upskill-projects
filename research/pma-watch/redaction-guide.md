# How to black out your documents before sharing them

The audit only needs amounts, dates, line descriptions and the agreement's terms. Remove everything else.

## Remove

- **Tenant names, phone numbers and emails.** These are your tenants' details, not yours to share.
- **Your bank account and routing numbers.**
- **The street address.** Replace it with "Property 1"; keep unit labels like A and B.
- **Your name and the manager's staff names**, if you prefer.
- **Signatures.**

## Keep

- Every date, amount and line description.
- Unit labels.
- The clause numbers and wording on fees, repairs, inspections, payments and renewals.
- The management company's name, if you're comfortable. It helps if the same company appears in several audits, but it's optional.

## How to do it properly

- **Don't just draw black boxes over a PDF.** In many PDF tools the text underneath can still be copied out.
- **Use a proper redaction tool:** Adobe Acrobat's Redact, or Preview on a Mac (Tools → Redact).
- **Or print, black out by hand, and photograph each page.**
- **Check your work:** open the redacted file, select all the text, paste it into a note, and confirm the hidden details are gone.

## Even simpler

Send only the agreement's clauses on fees, repairs, inspections, payments and renewals, plus the statements' line items typed into a spreadsheet with no names at all.
