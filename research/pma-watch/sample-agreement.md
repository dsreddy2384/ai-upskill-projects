# SAMPLE management agreement (made up, for demonstration only)

> **This is a fictional document.** The owner, manager, address and every number are invented to show how a PMA Watch audit works. It is not a real agreement, not a template to sign, and not legal advice.

**Parties:** Pat Owner ("Owner") and Example Property Management LLC ("Manager")
**Property:** 1418 Example Avenue, Units A and B, Sampleton (a duplex)
**Term:** 12 months from 1 January 2026, renewing yearly

Only the clauses the audit checks are shown.

## 4. Fees

4.1 **Management fee:** 9% of rent **actually collected** in each month.
4.2 **Leasing fee:** 50% of one month's rent when Manager places a new tenant.
4.3 **Lease renewal fee:** $150 per renewal.
4.4 **Maintenance coordination fee:** 10% of each vendor invoice Manager arranges.
4.5 **Inspection fee:** $75 per unit per inspection carried out under clause 7.

## 5. Repairs and approvals

5.1 Manager may approve a single repair of up to **$400** without asking Owner.
5.2 Any repair over $400 needs Owner's **written approval** (email is enough) before work starts.
5.3 **Emergencies** (a risk to people or serious damage to the property) may go ahead without approval. Manager must **notify Owner within 24 hours** and explain what was done.
5.4 Manager will post each vendor invoice to the owner portal within 5 business days of payment.

## 6. Money to Owner

6.1 Manager keeps a **$500 reserve** for repairs.
6.2 Manager pays Owner the remaining balance, with a monthly statement, **by the 12th of each month**.

## 7. Inspections

7.1 Manager's **own staff** will carry out an **interior inspection** of each unit **every six months**.
7.2 Manager will send Owner a **photo report within 7 days** of each inspection.

## 8. Renewals

8.1 At least **60 days before** a lease ends, Manager will send Owner a **recommended renewal rent** with **at least three comparable rents** nearby.
8.2 Manager will not sign a renewal without Owner's approval of the rent.
