# PMA Watch: first-week test kit

Everything needed to test PMA Watch by hand before writing code. The idea and its evidence are in `../ideas/2026-11-26.md`.

## "We don't have any work to show"

The sample is the work to show. It is a complete audit on made-up documents, clearly labelled as made up. Pair it with honesty: "I'm building this, I have no customers yet, so the first five are free." Don't invent results, testimonials or customer counts.

## Files

| File | What it is | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| `sample-agreement.md` | Fictional management agreement with the clauses the audit checks | Public |
| `sample-statements.md` | Three fictional monthly statements with seven planted problems | Public |
| `sample-audit-report.md` | The audit of the two: findings, limits, and a draft email to the manager | Public: link to this one |
| `do-it-yourself.md` | Checklist plus an AI prompt owners run themselves | Owners who won't share documents |
| `redaction-guide.md` | How to black out documents properly | Owners who share |
| `data-promise.md` | What happens to shared documents | Send before asking for anything |
| `outreach.md` | Forum post, thread reply, private message, and the go/stop tracker | You |

## Order of work

1. Publish the sample report and the do-it-yourself guide somewhere you can link to: a public page, or a Google Doc set to "anyone with the link can view".
2. Make the public post (outreach section 1). Answer every reply.
3. Two or three days later, reply in the owner threads and send the private messages.
4. Run the audits with Claude by hand. Check every dollar amount yourself.
5. Fill in the tracker. Decide go or stop at the end of week 2.

## Planted problems in the sample (answer key)

1. Water heater repaired, then replaced five weeks later (emergency notice not shown).
2. $640 repair over the $400 limit, marked routine, no approval shown.
3. $150 inspection fees for tenant-completed checklists.
4. Management fee on rent due, not collected: $49.50.
5. Coordination fee at 15% instead of 10%: $32.00.
6. Renewal signed with no recommendation shown.
7. September payment 12 days late.

Fees 4 and 5 are the kind of thing DoorVault's money audit also catches. Items 1, 2, 3 and 6 are where PMA Watch differs.
